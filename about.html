Our Story

A simple idea.
A bigger vision.

Hello, I'm Aniket Adhao, the founder of Patil Cold and an Electronics Engineer. I started Patil Cold with a vision to build a brand that brings delicious and affordable ice cream closer to every family.

For us, ice cream is more than a dessert. It is a reason to celebrate, a way to share a smile, and a small pleasure that makes ordinary days special.

Our ambition is to grow Patil Cold into a recognised Indian ice cream brand by focusing on taste, consistent quality, customer satisfaction, and value for money.

As we grow, we want to create products that people love, build lasting relationships with our customers, and bring happier moments to more homes across India.

Aniket Adhao Founder, Patil Cold
